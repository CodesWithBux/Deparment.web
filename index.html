Minister Of Finance

Enoch Godongwana

Education Background: Holds an MSc degree in Financial Economics from the University of London.

Role: Appointed Minister of Finance on 5 August 2021 and reappointed on 3 July 2024. He is responsible for tabling the national budget, managing public finances, overseeing tax policies (SARS), and implementing economic reforms to stabilize debt and promote growth.

Political Context:Member of the National Executive Committee of the African National Congress (ANC) and chairperson of the ANC's Economic Transformation sub-committee.



Deputy Minister of finance



Dr. David Masondo

Education Background: Obtained a PhD from New York University (USA) focusing on Post-Apartheid State Industrial Policy and Investment in the South African Automotive Industry, and holds a BA (Hons) and MA from Wits University.

Role: Appointed Deputy Minister of Finance on 30 May 2019 and reappointed on 3 July 2024. He leads the ministry's pro-growth structural economic reforms, chairs the Public Investment Corporation (PIC), and oversees the implementation of policies related to economic sectors.

Background: Former Finance MEC in Limpopo and CEO of the Automotive Industry Development Center.



Ashor Nick Sarupen

Education Background: Holds a Bachelor of Arts in Politics, Philosophy and Economics (PPE) from UNISA and a Bachelor of Social Science Honours in PPE from the University of Pretoria.

Role: Appointed Deputy Minister of Finance on 30 June 2024 as part of the Government of National Unity. He serves as a Deputy Minister from the Democratic Alliance (DA), providing oversight on the national budget and appropriations.

Background: Former DA Chief Whip in the Ekurhuleni Metropolitan Council, Member of the Gauteng Legislature, and Shadow Minister on the Appropriations Committee.
